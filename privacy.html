Privacy Policy

Handwrite Digitalizer ("the app") is a browser-based tool that transcribes handwritten text from photos and appends the result to a document you own. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Short version: The app has no servers, no database, and no user accounts. Your content never leaves your own devices and your own Google Drive. We collect nothing.

What the App Accesses

  • Photos you upload — processed locally in your browser and sent directly to Google's Gemini AI for transcription. They are not stored by this app on any server.
  • Your Google Drive — the app requests the drive.file permission, which grants access only to the specific file you select through Google's own file picker. The app cannot see, read, or access any other file in your Drive.
  • Your API keys — your Gemini API key and Google credentials are stored only on your device using your browser's localStorage. They are never transmitted to or stored by this app.

What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or any personal information.
  • We do not track your usage, clicks, or sessions.
  • We do not use cookies or third-party analytics.
  • We do not store your poems, photos, or transcriptions on any server.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize any data — because we have none.

Third-Party Services

The app sends your uploaded images to the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Gemini AI — receives your images for transcription. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Drive API — used to read and write the manuscript file you select. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

Draft history is stored in your browser's localStorage and remains entirely on your device. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings or within the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser data removes all locally stored information.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
